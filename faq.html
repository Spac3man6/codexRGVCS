Choosing A Finish

Staining, Polishing, Epoxy, And Decorative Coatings

Staining adds color and tonal variation to concrete that stays looking like concrete. Polishing grinds and refines existing concrete for reflectivity and an exposed, low-maintenance finish. Epoxy and other coating systems build a film layer on top of the concrete for stronger resistance and more uniform coverage. The right one depends on the look you want and how much protection the surface actually needs.

Decorative overlays are the right call when the concrete needs cosmetic correction, when an owner wants texture or a visual language that stain or polish cannot deliver, or when the existing slab is too damaged for a straightforward stain or polish job to look clean. They are not only for damaged concrete, but damaged concrete makes the case for them stronger.

Usually not the same system. A polished or stained interior floor and a residential garage face different demands, and a garage needs stronger film build, hot-tire resistance, and abrasion resistance that interior finishes are not built for. We treat them as separate systems even on the same property.
