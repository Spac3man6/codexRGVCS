# RGV Concrete Stain Branding Guide

## Direction

The site keeps the Craftsman-inspired structure from the reference analysis but adapts it for Rio Grande Valley decorative concrete work:

- Wide editorial sections instead of lightweight marketing blocks
- Strong horizontal rules and dense dark footer treatment
- Bold display type with restrained orange accents
- Gallery-first proof before long-form explanation

## Palette

- `--ink`: `#161616`
  - Primary text, borders, header depth, and structural contrast
- `--ink-soft`: `#2c2c2c`
  - Footer surfaces and secondary dark backgrounds
- `--slab`: `#d7d1c7`
  - Warm concrete-like support tone
- `--stone`: `#ece7df`
  - Light neutral panel background for cards and galleries
- `--sand`: `#f6f1e8`
  - Proposal and softer section background tone
- `--paper`: `#fffdf8`
  - Main page background
- `--accent`: `#d96b1d`
  - Primary CTA color and key highlight color
- `--accent-deep`: `#b95510`
  - Hover and pressed accent state
- `--accent-soft`: `rgba(217, 107, 29, 0.16)`
  - Focus and subtle accent wash

## Palette Rationale

- The neutral foundation keeps the site grounded in concrete, stone, and dust rather than generic SaaS whites
- The orange accent is used sparingly so calls to action still feel deliberate
- Warm off-whites prevent the site from feeling sterile while keeping enough contrast for editorial layouts

## Typography

- Display: `Bebas Neue`
  - Used for primary headings, section titles, and large branded statements
  - Rationale: condensed, assertive, industrial, and consistent with the analyzed reference hierarchy
- UI / Navigation: `Barlow Semi Condensed`
  - Used for nav, buttons, labels, chips, eyebrow text, and support metadata
  - Rationale: tighter uppercase treatment without feeling ornamental
- Body: `Barlow`
  - Used for paragraphs, explanatory content, and form copy
  - Rationale: readable, neutral, and adaptable across dense informational sections

## Tone And Voice

- Local and practical, not generic luxury language
- Focus on slab condition, prep, maintenance, and use case
- Designed to sound credible to homeowners, builders, and commercial operators without pretending to be a national franchise

## Layout Rules

- Use the full-width editorial rail already defined by `--container` and `--container-wide`
- Keep sections left-aligned on mobile; do not redesign into centered hero stacks
- Let imagery and proof do the persuasive work before longer explanatory copy
- Preserve repeated CTA rhythm: hero, mid-page form, footer, sticky CTA, modal
