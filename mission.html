How Mission Projects Run

A Local Sequence, From First Walkthrough To Sealed Surface

The system choice matters less than the prep and the exposure. This is the order of operations we use so a Mission patio or floor performs the same in August as it did on install day.

01

Walk The Slab

We meet at the property, check moisture, prior coatings, repairs, and exposure, and talk through how the space is actually used.

02

Match The System

Stain, polish, epoxy, or overlay gets recommended from slab condition and use case, not from whichever product is easiest to sell.

03

Plan Around The Season

Outdoor work gets scheduled around heat and rain windows. Winter-resident homes can be finished while the owner is away.

04

Seal For Sun And Water

Pool decks, full-sun patios, and interior floors each get a different sealer build, with traction and maintenance expectations put in writing.
