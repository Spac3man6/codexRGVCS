How Harlingen Projects Run

A Sequence Built For Business Hours And Older Concrete

Harlingen work splits between commercial space that cannot stay closed and older slabs with history under the surface. The same four steps handle both, and the schedule is set before anyone lifts a grinder.

Start With Photos

Photos, rough square footage, and how the space is used get us to a working number before anyone drives anywhere.
