How McAllen Projects Run

A Local Sequence, From First Walkthrough To Sealed Floor

The system choice matters less than the prep and the exposure. This is the order of operations we use so a McAllen floor performs the same in August as it did on install day.

01

Walk The Slab

We meet at the property, check moisture, prior coatings, repairs, and exposure, and talk through how the space is actually used.

02

Match The System

Stain, polish, epoxy, or overlay gets recommended from slab condition and use case, not from whichever product is easiest to sell.

03

Plan The Downtime

Homes get room-by-room phasing. Businesses get evening, weekend, or sectioned installs so the doors stay open where possible.

04

Seal For The Exposure

Full-sun patios, interior retail, and garage floors each get a different sealer build, with maintenance expectations put in writing.
